WHO WE ARE

Growing Together in Christ

The FBC Youth Fellowship is a community of young people committed to growing in faith, building meaningful relationships, serving God and making a positive impact in our community.

We believe that young people have an important role to play in the church and in society. Through fellowship, Bible study, prayer, outreach and service, we encourage one another to live out our faith everyday.

Our Mission

To help young people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ, discover their purpose, and use their gifts to server God and others.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of Christ centered young people who influence their church community and the world for God's glory.
